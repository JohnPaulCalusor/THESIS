Safety

Safety Details

  1. All participants shall observe the minimum health standards of the government and venue/hotel.
  2. Those who are sick or with visibly observed flu-like symptoms like cough and colds are advised to stay in their hotel room and inform their attending moderators/adviser and the organizer for proper disposition.
  3. All students shall observe the minimum health standards of the government and venue/hotel.
  4. Those who are sick or with visibly observed flu-like symptoms like cough and colds are advised to stay in their hotel room and inform their attending moderators/adviser and the organizer for proper disposition.
  5. Practitioners/Advisers should know their students’ important information, such as but not limited to the following: contact information of participants and emergency contact person, medical/health concern, food diet/allergies, etc.
  6. Risky stunts, and use of unsafe props during presentations are prohibited.
  7. The organizers shall not arrange for the participants’ transportation. Hence, the participants, especially the practitioners/advisers should make sure that participants go home safely after the activity (i.e. hiring a vehicle with valid licenses, coordinating with parents/guardians for pick-up, checking via phone call or text if students have arrived home).
  8. Organizers and participants should be aware of the hotel’s procedures in case of emergencies during the activity (e.g. fire and earthquake, etc.).
  9. If the student has any health concerns that may affect his/her participation in the activity, he/she must inform the attending practitioner/adviser, bring personal medicines and indicate contact persons for emergencies.
  10. For health and security emergencies, the participants should immediately coordinate with the organizers and hotel authorities.
  11. Participants are highly discouraged to leave the venue while the official IYF activities are ongoing, except for emergencies. Hence, they should free their schedule solely for the activity, and avoid arranging for a tour during the IYF schedule.
  12. The organizers shall not be responsible for the participants’ safety in their personal time and activities outside the official IYF Program. Exercise of due diligence, especially for the attending practitioners/ advisers, is strongly advised.
  13. Participants are responsible for all their belongings/valuables. The organizers shall not be liable for any loss or damage to the participants’ belongings/valuables.
  14. The PAPSAS, Inc shall not be held liable for any injury resulting from accidents caused by human error or equipment failure or by force majeure during the activity. Likewise, it shall not be held liable for the health and safety of the participants should he/she decide to leave the venue, especially if without the permission of the authorized school representative/s. It shall not be held liable for any untoward incident as a result of the participants’ non compliance with any rules and regulations of the school or event.

Issuance of Certificates and PAPSAS ID

Each delegate will receive the following electronic copy of certificates:

  1. Certificate of Participation
  2. Certificate of Appearance

The organizing committee will not issue physical copies of certificates in line with its commitment to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production) and SDG 13 (Climate Action). By opting for electronic certificates, we aim to reduce paper waste, minimize our carbon footprint, and promote sustainable practices. All certificates will be sent directly to each delegate’s registered email address. However, those delegates who are required to have originally signed CERTIFICATE OF APPEARANCE due to Accounting requirements will be provided with one. However, delegates who require an originally signed Certificate of Appearance for accounting or liquidation purposes will be provided with a physical copy.

Each delegate must register between 7:30 AM and 8:00 AM (Day 2 and 3) using the QR Code provided. To ensure the program runs on schedule, attendance during this period is mandatory. Delegates must complete daily registration for all three sessions to receive a Certificate of Attendance. E-certificates will be sent at the end of each day's sessions. Missing any session will result in non-issuance of the certificate for that day.
