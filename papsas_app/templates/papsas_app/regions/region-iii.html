PAPSAS Region 3 Oath-Taking Ceremony

Clark-Pampanga – On June 26, 2025, the Philippine Association of Practitioners of Student Affairs and Services (PAPSAS) in Region 3 conducted its official oath-taking ceremony for the newly elected officers and members of the Board of Directors at Hotel Seoul, Clark-Pampanga. The occasion marked a remarkable milestone in the history of the regional chapter, as it reaffirmed its unwavering commitment to advance student welfare and promote quality services within the academic community.

During the program, the incoming officers and directors solemnly pledged to uphold their duties with integrity and professionalism. The event underscored the steadfast participation of the membership body, whose collective effort is essential in sustaining the chapter’s initiatives.
