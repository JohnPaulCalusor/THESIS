PAPSAS Region 3 Oath-Taking Ceremony

Clark-Pampanga – On June 26, 2025, the Philippine Association of Practitioners of Student Affairs and Services (PAPSAS) in Region 3 conducted its official oath-taking ceremony for the newly elected officers and members of the Board of Directors at Hotel Seoul, Clark-Pampanga. The occasion marked a remarkable milestone in the history of the regional chapter, as it reaffirmed its unwavering commitment to advance student welfare and promote quality services within the academic community. The ceremony stood not only as a formal recognition of new leadership but also as a symbol of renewed dedication to the organization’s long-standing mission and vision.


During the program, the incoming officers and directors solemnly pledged to uphold their duties with integrity and professionalism, ensuring that their actions remain aligned with the values of PAPSAS. Their commitment reflected the broader goals of the association: to continuously support student affairs practitioners, provide platforms for growth, and foster collaboration across institutions. The event also underscored the steadfast participation of the membership body, whose collective effort and dedication are essential in sustaining the chapter’s initiatives. By honoring its leaders and highlighting the spirit of service among its members, PAPSAS Region 3 reinforced its role as a vital force in strengthening student affairs and services across the region.

19th PAPSAS III Annual Convention & Student Leaders’ Summit

The 19th PAPSAS III Annual Convention and Student Leaders’ Summit will be held at Galilee Resort in Bustos, Bulacan on May 15 to 16, 2025 with the theme “Harmonizing Leadership Challenges and Personal Well-Being.” Warm greetings from the PAPSAS III Board of Directors. As a CHED-recognized professional organization, the Philippine Association of Practitioners of Student Affairs and Services in Region III continues to champion student development by empowering SAS practitioners and student leaders. This year’s convention aims to address challenges in student affairs work while emphasizing personal well-being. Student leaders can look forward to dynamic team-building activities, while SAS practitioners will connect through the Kapehan at Kumustahan session.


Pre-registration is encouraged. The early-bird rate is Php 4,000 with a deadline of April 25, 2025. The regular rate is Php 4,500. Fees are inclusive of accommodation, meals, speaker honoraria, and certificates. Register now at https://forms.gle/R55F1qdwRqrbms1CA and view more details at bit.ly/19thPAPSAS3summit. Payments may be made through BPI Morayta Branch under the account name Philippine Association of Practitioners of Student Affairs and Services, Inc., account number 1581-0025-44. Please send your proof of payment and CHED CMO No. 63 series of 2017 compliance documents. Official receipts will be issued onsite. For inquiries, message us on Facebook or email papsasregion03@gmail.com. Let us come together to grow, connect, and thrive as student affairs professionals and leaders. See you there!

SAS Reorientation: Legal Bases & Provisions

The Philippine Association of Practitioners of Student Affairs and Services in Region III (PAPSAS III) invites all Student Affairs and Services practitioners to the convention titled “SAS Reorientation: Legal Bases and Other Provisions in the Implementation of Student Affairs and Services,” to be held on March 20, 2025 at Casa Nena, Holy Angel University, Angeles City, Pampanga. The event aims to foster engagement and collaboration by sharing best practices and updates on the legal bases and provisions relevant to SAS work. Register by scanning the QR code or visiting bit.ly/RegSASReorientation. We are also calling for presenters who wish to share best practices in implementing SAS programs; please review the format and mechanics at bit.ly/Format-Mechanics.


The registration fee is Php 1,200.00, inclusive of meals, convention kits, materials, speaker honoraria, and venue. Slots are limited to a maximum of 100 participants. A sales invoice will be issued onsite, and an advance or scanned copy is available upon request. Payment details are indicated in the registration link at bit.ly/RegSASReorientation. For more information, including the program and registration guidelines, visit bit.ly/SASreorientation2025. For inquiries, email papsasregion03@gmail.com or message PAPSAS Region 3 on Facebook. Do not miss this opportunity to collaborate, learn, and enhance our SAS implementation together. See you there!

18th PAPSAS III Annual Convention & Student Leader Summit

Reflecting on the incredible 18th PAPSAS III Annual Convention and Student Leader Summit held on June 13 to 14, 2024 at Palm Cabanas Resort in Arayat, Pampanga, with the theme “Strategic Directions Toward Better SAS.” We extend our heartfelt gratitude to Dr. Renelyn Salcedo, PAPSAS National President, for her support, and to Prof. Winifredo Luis, former head of the Student Discipline Unit and president of the TSU Federated Faculty Union, for an engaging keynote address.


We also thank Dr. Lora Yusi, Director IV of CHEDRO III, for her inspiring recorded plenary talk. Our appreciation goes to the SAS practitioners who shared best practices that featured economic enterprises, and to the student leaders who presented outstanding community engagement programs. To all delegates, thank you for your meaningful participation that made the event a resounding success. We look forward to more enriching experiences together. Credits to John Daniel Sarmiento, Ezekiel Refuncion, and Micaela Padilla for the photos.
