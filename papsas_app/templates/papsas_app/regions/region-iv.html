PAPSAS Region IV & CHED Region IV Collaborative Conference

PAPSAS Region IV and CHED Region IV will hold a Collaborative Conference on November 28, 2025 at Batangas State University, The National Engineering University, along Rizal Avenue in Batangas City.

With the theme “Reviving and Strengthening Student Welfare and Legal Awareness in Higher Education in Region IV,” this one-day event aims to empower SAS practitioners across the region and reinforce our shared commitment to student welfare, advocacy, and holistic development within higher education institutions.

Register and view the full program at: https://bit.ly/48VrLE6 | http://bit.ly/4nSfi8H

IN PHOTOS: 29th PAPSAS National Conference and Training Workshop

Take a look back at the vibrant moments captured during the 29th PAPSAS National Conference and Training Workshop, held last April 10–12, 2025 at Costa Palawan Resort, Puerto Princesa City!
