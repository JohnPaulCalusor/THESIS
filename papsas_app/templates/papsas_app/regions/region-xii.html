PAPSAS XI Elects Board of Trustees and Executive Officers for AY 2025-2026

The Philippine Association of Practitioners of Student Affairs and Services, Inc. Region XI Chapter (PAPSAS XI) successfully elected its Board of Trustees and Executive Officers for Academic Year 2025-2026, reaffirming its continuing dedication to participatory governance, professional integrity, and excellence in student affairs and services across Philippine higher education institutions.

The election was the last of the Business Meeting agenda during the 5th PAPSAS XI General Assembly and Conference held on September 26, 2025 at Luxebridge Hotel, Barangay Ma-a, Davao City. The event theme was “Click. Connect. Care: Student Affairs and Services in the Age of Digital Media.”

The Election Committee, chaired by Mr. Irvin N. Mirabueno (Vice President for Student Affairs and Services, South Philippine Adventist College), oversaw the orderly and transparent conduct of elections. Assisting him were Ms. Janice N. Sipin (Assumption College of Nabunturan, Inc.) and Ms. Venus R. Bonsubre (North Davao Colleges, Inc.).
